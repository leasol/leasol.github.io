Account and Data Deletion

App: 2026 Quiz: Celebs Game

Developer: Lea Sol Bokobza

Last Updated: June 29, 2026

This page explains how users of 2026 Quiz: Celebs Game can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Email soldarcompany@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my 2026 Quiz: Celebs Game account.

In your email, include the email address used for VIP cloud sync or account sign-in in the app. If you contact us from a different email address, we may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it.

What Data Is Deleted

After verifying your request, we delete account data associated with your app sign-in, including:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account for this app, if one exists
  • Cloud-synced VIP progress data linked to your account email
  • Cloud-synced gameplay data such as solved questions, unlocked levels, coins, and similar progress data linked to your account
  • Support messages linked to the deletion request, except where retention is needed for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons

What Data May Be Kept

Some data may be retained when deletion is not controlled by the app developer or when retention is required for legitimate reasons:

  • Purchase and subscription records handled by Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, or payment providers may be retained according to their policies and legal requirements.
  • Advertising, analytics, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention data collected by third-party SDKs such as Google AdMob or Firebase may be retained by those providers according to their own policies.
  • Minimal records may be kept when required to comply with tax, accounting, legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Deletion Timeline and Retention Period

We aim to complete verified account deletion requests within 30 days. Backup copies and logs may take up to 90 days to be fully removed from routine backup systems.

Data that must be retained for legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons may be kept for as long as required or permitted by applicable law.
