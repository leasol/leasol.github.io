Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Lea Sol Bokobza (“Lea”, “I”, “me”, or “my”) develops and publishes mobile game titles (“Games”) for devices through Google Play and the Apple App Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and share your information when you use my Games. I take your privacy seriously and aim to be transparent about my data practices.

About Me

I am an independent game developer. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile Games developed and published by Lea Sol Bokobza, whether available on Android or iOS platforms.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected and processed through my Games. It does not apply to other websites, services, or third-party content that may be linked within the Games.

Because my Games are available globally, this policy is designed to comply with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the EU/EEA, UK, and Switzerland
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for residents of California, USA

Information I Collect

1. Information Collected Automatically Through Games

This includes information collected directly through the app or via analytics and advertising SDKs:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / IDFA / IDFV)
  • IP address
  • Device type, OS, and version
  • App version and usage data
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data
  • In-game interactions and session length
  • Transaction and purchase data (if applicable)
  • Language and time zone
  • Mobile carrier and network type

2. Information from Advertising & Analytics Partners

  • Ad interactions and view data
  • Attributed ad revenue
  • User behavior profiles (for personalized ads if consented)

How I Use the Information

I may use your information for the following purposes:

  • Operate and improve the Games
  • Analyze usage and performance
  • Fix bugs and enhance functionality
  • Serve in-game ads (personalized if allowed)
  • Provide support and respond to inquiries
  • Prevent fraud and ensure app security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Maximum Information Retention Period

Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes listed above, unless a longer period is legally required or permitted.

How I Share Your Information

I may share data with the following:

  • Ad networks & marketing partners (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads)
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Firebase, GameAnalytics)
  • Third-party service providers (e.g., crash reporting, storage)
  • Legal authorities, if required to comply with laws

How to Control Your Information

Request Account and Data Deletion

If you use account features such as VIP cloud sync, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by following the steps on the Account and Data Deletion page.

Opt-out of Personalized Ads

You can limit personalized ads via your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > Turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

GDPR Rights

If you're located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability (in certain cases)
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact me at: soldarcompany@gmail.com

CCPA Rights

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what data is collected and how it's used
  • Request deletion of their data
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data (Note: I do not sell data)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights

To make a request, email: soldarcompany@gmail.com

Children’s Privacy

I do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). If you believe your child provided personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside of your own. I follow applicable laws and implement safeguards for international data transfers.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated within the app or store listing. The “Last Updated” date reflects the latest revision.

Logo Quiz and MindBlow privacy details

Logo Quiz (com.soldar.logoquiz) and MindBlow (com.soldar.mindblow) use Google AdMob mediation with Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, and InMobi. These providers may collect and share device or account identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising, analyze performance, provide SDK functionality, and prevent fraud.

On Android, versions that include location permission may also allow the InMobi advertising SDK to collect and share precise location when location access is granted. Location access is optional: you can deny or revoke it in Android Settings > Apps > the app > Permissions. The games do not require precise location to play. Advertising consent choices remain available through the in-app Ad privacy control where required.

MindBlow uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchased content, and analyze purchase activity. This involves purchase history and a generated app user identifier. Advertising SDKs may also process purchase information as described in their own policies. Logo Quiz additionally uses Firebase for optional sign-in, cloud progress, leaderboards, and push notifications; data supplied to these features can include your email address, user ID, display name, gameplay progress, and notification identifiers.

For Logo Quiz or MindBlow data deletion requests, email soldarcompany@gmail.com with the app name and a description of the data you want deleted. If you used Logo Quiz cloud sign-in, include the sign-in email so the account can be identified and ownership verified. Do not send passwords or payment card details. MindBlow does not provide a separate game sign-in account; purchase and advertising records may need to be identified through their respective providers. Requests concerning data controlled by those providers are handled under their deletion procedures. Records required for legal, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention purposes may be retained.

Famous Celebrity Birthdays privacy details

Famous Celebrity Birthdays (com.soldar.celebritybirthdays) uses Google AdMob mediation with Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, and InMobi. Advertising providers collect and share device and SDK user identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, SDK functionality, and fraud prevention. Unity Ads also discloses processing purchase information for advertising and analytics; the app does not offer a separate purchase or sign-in account.

Android versions containing location permission may allow the InMobi SDK to collect and share precise location for advertising when location access is granted. Precise location is optional and is not needed to use the app. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings > Apps > Famous Celebrity Birthdays > Permissions. If you choose to search for a celebrity, your search query is sent to The Movie Database to provide results. The app also stores aggregate tribute counts. Local preferences and notification reminders remain on your device unless a feature described here transmits them. There is no app account creation or login flow.

To request deletion of Famous Celebrity Birthdays data, email soldarcompany@gmail.com with the app name, the celebrity and tribute text involved, and a description of the data you want removed. Do not send passwords or payment details. I can remove identifiable tribute content under my control; aggregated records that cannot identify a person may remain. Advertising or search data controlled by third-party providers is subject to their own deletion procedures. Data is retained only as needed for the stated purposes, including applicable legal, security, and fraud-prevention obligations; no automatic 90-day deletion is promised.
